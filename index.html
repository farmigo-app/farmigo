Common questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open More and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your farm records.

I didn't get the verification email.
Check your spam folder, then use Resend email on the verification screen.

I forgot my password.
On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link sent to your email.

Can I use Farmigo without internet?
Yes. Records you add, edit or delete offline are saved on your phone and sync automatically when you are back online. Signing in, signing up and deleting your account need internet. Don't uninstall the app or clear its data before it syncs, or offline changes will be lost.

How do I record an egg sale?
In Finance tap +, choose Record Income and pick Egg Sales. Tap Trays Sold and set the trays for each size (Max fills all trays left). Only full trays in stock can be sold.

Where can I see my egg stock?
Open Eggs and tap the Egg Stock card. It shows trays left per size, your egg sales, and a + to record a sale. Low stock means 1 to 5 trays left; Out of stock means no full tray.

What do the flock stage tags mean?
They follow the flock's age: Brooding (0–5 weeks), Growing (6–17), Starting to lay (18–24), Peak laying (25–40), Laying (41–71) and Ready to cull (72+ weeks).

Delete your account

In the app: open More and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app: email farmigo.support@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Farmigo account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your sign-in account and all of your farm records – flocks, egg collections, feed, mortality, finance records and activity history. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep it.
